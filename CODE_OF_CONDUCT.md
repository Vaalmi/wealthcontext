# Code of conduct

WealthContext follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
where someone is acting for the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good faith, and criticise the work, not the
  person.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualised language or attention, or
  publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, write to
**contact@vaalmi.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. The
maintainers will review each report, decide what to do, which may range from a private
word to removal from the project's spaces, and tell the reporter the outcome.

Security problems are reported differently; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
